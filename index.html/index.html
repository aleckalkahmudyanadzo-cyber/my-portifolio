About

From coursework to systems that actually run

I am Aleck Mudyanadzo, a Computer Science student at Midlands State University. My focus is finishing strong academically while building a body of work I can show to employers, in Zimbabwe and beyond.

Outside the classroom, I am working to move from being someone who simply uses technology to someone who builds it. That means going deeper into machine learning, data engineering, cloud and GPU computing, Linux and networking, containerisation, MLOps, applied security, computer vision, and large language model engineering, one layer at a time.

I also trade financial markets and am applying that same systems thinking to an algorithmic trading project, alongside a personal social platform and a set of portfolio projects built specifically with Zimbabwean companies and institutions in mind.
